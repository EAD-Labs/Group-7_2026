The Reality of Indian Public Schools

Why Existing Assessment Systems Fail

Formative assessment produces dramatic learning gains when feedback is immediate. But in Zilla Parishad and municipal government schools, teachers face severe infrastructure barriers.

📄

Why Paper OMR Sheets Fail

Bubble sheets require recurring paper, printing, and toner budgets for every single quiz. Grading requires high-resolution scanners or batch computer processing that takes 24–48 hours, eliminating any opportunity for same-day remedial teaching.

📱

Why Tablets & Clickers Fail

Equipping 40+ students with individual hardware clickers or tablets requires thousands of dollars in upfront capital. In rural schools, hardware suffers from dead batteries, charging logistics, device breakage, and severe theft risks.

🙋

Why Hand-Raising Fails

Asking students to raise hands results in extreme peer-mimicry bias: hesitant learners look around and blindly copy the class topper. Teachers cannot identify who is truly confused, shy students stay silent, and zero digital records are kept.

The FAST Innovation

How Pi Jam FAST Works in 3 Steps

A seamless physical-digital bridge that requires zero student electronics and works entirely offline.

1

Students Rotate Their Card

Each student receives a durable laminated card. The card features 4 colored edges: A (Red), B (Yellow), C (Blue), and D (Green). When a question is asked, students hold up their card with their chosen answer edge pointing UP toward the ceiling.

2

Teacher Sweeps Phone Camera

The teacher opens the Pi Jam FAST app on their regular smartphone and sweeps the camera across the classroom in one smooth 5–15 second motion. The computer vision engine localizes and decodes every student's card simultaneously.

3

Instant Actionable Analytics

The screen displays real-time Augmented Reality boxes, live counters for missing students, question accuracy percentages, distractor analysis (common misconceptions), and mastery scorecards ready for Excel/PDF export.

Human-Centered Design

Designed for Real Classroom Stakeholders

Tailored to the unique psychological, operational, and managerial needs of government education.

👩‍🏫 For Teachers

Eliminate grading fatigue while getting immediate diagnostic clarity before moving to the next lesson.

  • Zero manual grading: Complete a 5-question comprehension check in under 3 minutes total.
  • Live missing indicator: Instantly see which students haven't held up their cards without roll-calling.
  • Instant distractor analysis: Immediately discover if 70% of the class fell for distractor 'B'.
  • Runs on personal smartphone: No specialized hardware to carry or calibrate.

👦 For Students

High-engagement, low-anxiety active participation that protects student privacy and dignity.

  • Peer-blind voting: Back of card is mirrored; neighbors cannot see which option is pointing up.
  • No gadget anxiety: No fear of dropping expensive screens or dealing with password logins.
  • Equal classroom voice: Back-benchers and quiet students are heard equally in every sweep.
  • Durable & reusable: Heavyweight laminated cards withstand daily school handling.

📊 For Supervisors & Pi Jam

Transparent, longitudinal learning data across municipal schools and foundation clusters.

  • Zero PII exposure: Student records use privacy-safe composite keys (School Code + Class ID + Roll No).
  • Offline-first sync: Teachers scan without internet; sessions cache locally and upload when online.
  • Excel & PDF exports: One-click generation of formatted mastery reports for administration.
  • Supervisor Dashboard: Cluster-level views of question difficulty and teacher pacing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Addressing key technical and operational questions about deployment in public schools.

Does the classroom need Wi-Fi or active internet?

No. The entire computer vision scanning pipeline and SQLite database run locally. In offline mode, assessments are stored on the device and can be synchronized whenever the teacher reaches cellular coverage.

What happens if a student holds their card at a 45° angle?

The vision algorithm includes a built-in deadband filter. If the vertical coordinate difference between the top two edges is less than 15% of the card height, the reading is rejected as ambiguous, preventing false votes.

Can students cheat by looking at their classmate's card?

The back of each card (the student-facing side) is mirrored and contains minimal edge color boxes. From the side or back, classmates cannot easily decipher which option is oriented upward, preventing peer pressure and mimicry.

Do we need to print new cards for every class?

No! Cards are numbered Roll 1 to Roll 45 and laminated. Card #14 resolves to Roll #14 of whichever class is active (e.g., Grade 7-A in period 1, Grade 8-B in period 2). The same physical deck serves the entire school year after year.
